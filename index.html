Jiaxuan Zou （邹嘉轩）

Email · Google Scholar · X · LinkedIn · Bilibili · Xiaohongshu · WeChat: Jiaxuan_Zou

I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics and Statistics at Xi’an Jiaotong University, and currently with the Moonshot AI (Kimi) Pre-training team. Previously, I was a research intern in the ByteDance Seed Pre-training team and at the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Yong Liu.

My research centers on pre-training and scaling. I study how model architectures, data, and optimization jointly determine learning efficiency, training stability, and model capabilities. My approach connects empirical training phenomena with mathematical analysis and controlled experiments, aiming to make large-scale training more predictable.

I write research notes on my blog and welcome conversations on these topics. I am interested in future research opportunities in LLM pre-training and AI theory. More background: English / 中文.

Research Interests

  • Scaling laws and predictable large-scale pre-training
  • Joint design of model architectures, data, and optimizers
  • Feature learning, finite-width training dynamics, and hyperparameter transfer
